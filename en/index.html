Everything you check every day

Built around the questions publishers actually ask.

Clear daily dashboard

Today, yesterday, this month and last month — each compared with a fair baseline, plus impressions, eCPM, requests and match rate.

Explainable forecast

A 7-day forecast from weekday averages. Tap it to see exactly how it was calculated.

Where revenue comes from

Share by ad unit, app, country, format, platform, ad network and mediation group, as a donut or a sortable table.

Earnings calendar

Daily earnings on a calendar with monthly total, daily average and best day; tap any day for its per-app breakdown.

All your accounts

Connect AdMob and AdSense publishers from several Google accounts, see AdSense earnings by domain, and view all accounts combined.

Report, widget, currency

Optional 9 AM daily report, a home screen widget, and a secondary currency next to USD (29 currencies, ECB rates).

Privacy by design

  • Requests read-only Google scopes only: admob.readonly and adsense.readonly.
  • Earnings data and sign-in information are stored only on your device. There is no AdBoard server.
  • Never asks for permission to change ad settings or serve ads.
